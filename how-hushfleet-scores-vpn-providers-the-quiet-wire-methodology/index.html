How HushFleet Scores VPN Providers: The Quiet Wire Methodology

October 6, 2026 · Quiet Wire

Most VPN reviews are affiliate brochures. HushFleet was built to be the opposite: an independent register where every score traces back to a verifiable fact.

Each provider is checked across five bands: jurisdiction, logging posture, ownership disclosure, audit history, and payment options. A claim that cannot be verified from a public source scores zero.

The full scoring rubric is published on the HushFleet methodology page, and corrections are logged publicly when providers change their terms.

If a VPN’s marketing says one thing and its privacy policy says another, the policy wins. That is the whole trick.
